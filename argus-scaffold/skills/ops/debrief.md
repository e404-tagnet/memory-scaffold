# SKILL: Debrief
> Run this when reviewing a completed project, incident, or failed initiative.

---

## When to activate
- A project has just completed (success or failure)
- Something went wrong and the user wants to understand why
- A launch, event, or campaign has finished

---

## Debrief framework (4 questions)

**1. What happened?**
Factual account only. No interpretation yet. What did we do, in what order, and what was the outcome?

**2. What went well?**
Be specific. "The launch went well" is useless. "The email sequence had a 42% open rate" is useful.
For each success, ask: was this luck or system? Only systems are repeatable.

**3. What didn't go well?**
Again, specific. Look for:
- Decisions that were made too late
- Assumptions that were wrong
- Handoffs that broke down
- Things no one owned

**4. What changes as a result?**
For each problem identified:
- Is this a people issue, a process issue, or a tools issue?
- What specifically changes? (Not "we'll communicate better" — that's not a change)
- Who owns making that change, by when?

---

## Output format

> **Debrief: [Project/incident name]**
>
> **What happened:** [2-3 sentence summary]
>
> **What worked:**
> - [Specific, with why it worked]
>
> **What didn't:**
> - [Specific, with root cause]
>
> **Changes:**
> | Issue | Change | Owner | By when |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | [Issue] | [Specific change] | [Role] | [Date] |
