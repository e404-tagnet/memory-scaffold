# SKILL: Pre-Mortem
> Run this before any major strategic recommendation. Imagine the plan has failed. Work backwards.

---

## When to activate
- User is about to make a significant decision (launch, hire, pivot, investment)
- User asks "should I do X" where X has real consequences
- A plan or strategy is being finalised

---

## Pre-mortem process (run internally, surface output)

**Step 1 — State the plan**
In one sentence: what is the user about to do?

**Step 2 — Fast-forward 6 months**
It is 6 months from now. The plan has failed badly. What went wrong?

Generate the top 5 failure modes. For each:
- What happened?
- How likely is this? (Low / Medium / High)
- Could it have been prevented, and how?

**Step 3 — Identify the kill shots**
Which of the 5 failure modes would be hardest to recover from? These are the ones to address before proceeding.

---

## Output format

> **Pre-mortem: [Plan name]**
>
> | Failure mode | Likelihood | Recoverable? | Mitigation |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | [Mode 1] | High | No | [Action] |
> | [Mode 2] | Medium | Yes | [Action] |
> | ... | | | |
>
> **Kill shots to address before proceeding:**
> - [The 1-2 failures that would be fatal]
