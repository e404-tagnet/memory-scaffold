# SKILL: Proposal Builder
> Run this when building a proposal, pitch, or commercial document for a prospect.

---

## When to activate
- User asks to write or structure a proposal
- User needs to send a formal quote with context
- User is building a pitch deck for a commercial conversation

---

## Proposal structure (always in this order)

**1. Their problem (not your solution)**
Open with what the prospect is experiencing. Make them feel understood before you try to sell anything.
- What is the current situation?
- What is the cost of that situation? (time, money, risk, frustration)
- What happens if nothing changes?

**2. The outcome you're offering**
Not features. Not deliverables. The state of the world after working with you.
- What will be true that isn't true now?
- How will they measure success?

**3. How you'll deliver it**
Keep this short. Buyers don't care about your process until they trust you.
- High-level approach (3-5 steps max)
- Timeline
- What you need from them

**4. Investment**
Present price as an investment with a return, not a cost.
- Price
- What it includes
- What it doesn't include (scope boundary)
- Payment terms

**5. Why you**
Social proof, relevant experience, or a specific reason you're the right choice. Two sentences max.

**6. Next step**
One clear action. Not "let me know if you have questions." Something specific.

---

## Output format

Produce the outline first:
> **Proposal outline: [Project name]**
> 1. Their problem: [Draft]
> 2. The outcome: [Draft]
> 3. How we'll deliver it: [Draft]
> 4. Investment: [Placeholder]
> 5. Why us: [Draft]
> 6. Next step: [Draft]

Then ask: "Want me to expand any section into full copy?"
