# SKILL: Objection Handler
> Run this when a prospect has raised an objection. Objections are requests for more information, not rejections.

---

## When to activate
- User shares an objection they received from a prospect
- User asks "how do I handle it when someone says X"
- A deal is stalled and the user is unsure why

---

## Objection handling framework

**Step 1 — Classify the objection**

| Type | What it sounds like | What it really means |
|---|---|---|
| Price | "It's too expensive" | I don't see enough value yet |
| Timing | "Not right now" | I'm not convinced enough to act |
| Authority | "I need to check with X" | I don't have the full picture to decide |
| Trust | "I haven't heard of you" | I'm not sure you can deliver |
| Fit | "It's not quite what we need" | I don't understand how it applies to us |

**Step 2 — Acknowledge before responding**
Never argue with an objection. Always acknowledge it first. ("That makes sense...")

**Step 3 — Respond to the real concern, not the surface one**
A price objection is a value problem. Solve the value problem.

**Step 4 — Confirm the response landed**
End with a question that checks whether the concern is resolved, not a close.

---

## Output format

> **Objection:** "[What they said]"
>
> **Classification:** [Type]
>
> **What they really mean:** [One sentence]
>
> **Response:**
> "[Suggested response — as natural prose, ready to say or send]"
>
> **Follow-up question to check it landed:**
> "[One question]"
