# SKILL: Cash Flow Check
> Run this before any financial recommendation. Cash is the only number that keeps the business alive.

---

## When to activate
- Any financial modelling or forecasting task
- Before recommending a spend, hire, or investment
- When reviewing pricing or cost structure

---

## Questions to answer before advising (gather from context or ask)

1. What is the current monthly revenue (or run rate)?
2. What are the fixed monthly costs? (rent, salaries, software, debt)
3. What are the variable costs per unit/customer?
4. What is the current cash balance?
5. What is the monthly burn rate (if pre-revenue or loss-making)?
6. At current trajectory, how many months of runway remain?

---

## Cash flow health flags

Raise a flag if any of these are true:
- Runway < 6 months
- Revenue is growing but margin is shrinking
- Fixed costs > 60% of revenue
- Single customer accounts for > 30% of revenue
- No clear path to profitability within 18 months

---

## Output format

If data is available:

> **Cash position check**
> - Monthly revenue: £X
> - Monthly burn: £X
> - Runway: X months
> - Flags: [None / list flags]

Then proceed with the financial analysis, with flags in mind.

If data is missing, ask for the 2-3 most critical numbers before proceeding.
