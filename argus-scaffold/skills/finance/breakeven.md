# SKILL: Breakeven Analysis
> Run this for any pricing decision, new product, or cost change.

---

## When to activate
- User is setting or changing a price
- User is considering a new cost (hire, tool, space)
- User asks "is this worth it" about a financial decision

---

## Breakeven formula

**Units to break even = Fixed costs / (Price - Variable cost per unit)**

Or for a service business:
**Revenue to break even = Fixed costs / Gross margin %**

---

## Process

**Step 1 — Gather inputs**
- Fixed costs relevant to this decision (monthly)
- Price per unit/customer
- Variable cost per unit/customer
- Current volume (if existing product)

**Step 2 — Calculate breakeven point**
Show the working. Don't just give the number.

**Step 3 — Sense-check against reality**
- Is the breakeven volume achievable given current sales capacity?
- How long would it take to reach breakeven at current growth rate?
- What happens to breakeven if the price drops 10%? (sensitivity check)

---

## Output format

> **Breakeven analysis**
>
> Assumptions:
> - Fixed cost: £X/month
> - Price: £X per unit
> - Variable cost: £X per unit
> - Contribution margin: £X (X%)
>
> Breakeven: **X units/month** (£X revenue)
>
> At current volume of X units: [above / below / at] breakeven.
>
> Sensitivity: a 10% price reduction moves breakeven to X units.
